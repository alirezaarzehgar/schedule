# Schedule

This is my LaTeX template for a university schedule. I love it for its maintainability, simplicity, and perfection.
My love for LaTeX and Git compelled me to share this template. Feel free to fork, edit, and use it for anything.


You just need a LaTeX distribution like `texlive` or `miktex`. Remember, the Makefile is your friend;
use it to compile your schedule quickly and save your own PDF output.
